## 2. Cooling-off period (ECTA section 44)

Because you buy online, you may cancel your purchase **within 7 days of receiving the
Products, without giving a reason and without penalty**. We will refund the full
purchase price within **30 days** of cancellation. You are responsible for the cost of
returning the Products, and they must be returned in a resaleable condition.

## 3. Change-of-mind returns (our 30-day policy)

In addition to your cooling-off rights, we accept change-of-mind returns within
**30 days of delivery**, provided the Products are:

- unworn and unwashed, with all original tags attached;
- in their original packaging; and
- accompanied by proof of purchase (order number or receipt).

For change-of-mind returns you are responsible for return shipping costs. Once we
receive and inspect the Products, we will issue a **refund** or, if you prefer, an
**exchange**.

## 4. Defective, damaged or incorrect Products (CPA)

If Products are defective, damaged on arrival, or not as described, the CPA entitles
you to return them within **6 months** of delivery and to choose a **repair,
replacement or full refund**. We will also cover the cost of returning the Products in
these cases. We may inspect the Products to confirm the fault.

This does not apply to defects arising from normal wear and tear, misuse, or failure
to follow care instructions.
